EVO Starter Routine

One set · ~8–12 mins · 3×/week · keep it easy
1. Dumbbell Squat — Handles
8 reps
Legs + glutes + some core. Start light.

2. Bent Over Row — Handles
8 reps
Upper back + biceps. Keep your back neutral and movement controlled.

3. Bicep Curl — Handles
8 reps
Simple arm work. Elbows stay near your sides.
Open on YouTube
4. Standing Overhead Press — Handles
6–8 reps
Shoulders + triceps + core stability. Don’t lean back to finish the rep.
Open on YouTube
5. Glute Bridge — Bodyweight
10 reps
Put the EVO away. Lie on your back, knees bent, squeeze your glutes and lift your hips.
6. Dead Bug — Bodyweight
4–5 / side
Slow and controlled. Only extend as far as you can without your lower back arching off the floor.
Band rule for now
Use the lightest sensible pair for your first few sessions. If you finish a set and could comfortably do another 3–5 reps, that’s perfect. If form gets messy or you’re straining hard, go lighter.
